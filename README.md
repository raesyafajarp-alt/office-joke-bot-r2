# One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off — Round 2

make.fun competition: `u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73`
(https://make.fun/c/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73)

Round 1 entry (rank 1785/1945, score 344645) was the office-plant /
HR-condolence joke. This round 2 re-entry takes a different angle:
instead of mourning something in the office, it attacks the ritual
everyone recognises — the daily standup and its sacred time limit.

## Entry (115 characters, one line)

> Our standup has a strict 15-minute limit, so we spend 14 of them arguing about whose turn it is to start the timer.

## Why it should score better

- **Recognisable ritual, not a prop joke.** Almost everyone who works
  in an office has sat in a standup; far fewer have an office plant
  with an HR backstory.
- **The rule eats itself.** The 15-minute limit exists to save time,
  yet 14 of those minutes are spent arguing about the timer — the
  discipline is the waste.
- **Tight.** Numerals and one active verb ("arguing") keep the sentence
  short enough to land in a single breath, with the punch in the last
  clause.
